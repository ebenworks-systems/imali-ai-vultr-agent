    How your agent checks itself

    A reasoning model picks what to do, and code checks the rest. An amount must be one you said, a contractor must be in the books, and a payment can never be more than what is open.

    Every message and voice note passes Vultr's content-safety model first, and voice notes are turned into words by Vultr's speech model.

    Close my month
